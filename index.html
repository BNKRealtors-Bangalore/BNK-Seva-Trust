About BNK Seva Trust

Seva Beyond Boundaries

BNK Seva Trust is a movement of compassion inspired by the teachings of Baba Neem Karoli Maharaj. Through meaningful initiatives in education, healthcare, food distribution, community welfare, spiritual activities and environmental responsibility, the Trust seeks to make a positive difference in society.

Our Inspiration

Inspired by Baba Neem Karoli Maharaj

Baba Neem Karoli Maharaj's life was a living message of love, simplicity and oneness. He reminded us that true spirituality lies in seeing God in everyone and serving all with a compassionate heart.

A deep devotee of Lord Hanuman, Baba showed the power of unwavering faith, reminding us that surrender, love and service open the way to inner freedom.

His teachings continue to inspire BNK Seva Trust in its journey of seva - serving people, uplifting lives and spreading love, just as Baba lived and showed.
